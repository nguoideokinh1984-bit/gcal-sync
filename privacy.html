Privacy Policy

gcal-sync is a personal tool used only by its owner.

  • It accesses Google Calendar data of the owner's own account solely to create and update the owner's calendar events.
  • No data is collected from other users, sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • OAuth tokens are stored locally on the owner's machine and can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
